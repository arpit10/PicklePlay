How each format works

Use this during the session whenever someone needs a refresher.

🔄

Round Robin

Individual

Partners rotate between rounds. Each player earns their own wins, points for and point differential.

  • Play the scheduled match on your assigned court.
  • Enter the final score after every game.
  • Standings rank players individually.
🤝

Fixed Partners

Team

Choose a partner and stay together for the session. Teams rotate through opponents.

  • Your team stays intact every round.
  • Team results determine the standings.
👑

King / Queen / Royal Court

Team movement

Winners move toward the top court and losers move down. On the current top court, choose Challenge or Split before play.

  • Challenge: qualifying pairs stay together.
  • Split: the four top-court players are remixed.
  • The final top-court winning pair earns the crown.
⚖️

Balanced Scramble

Individual

Partners change while the app uses DUPR when available to make the two teams as balanced as possible.

🔥

Shootout

Individual promotion / relegation

Each stage contains two games per court. The four players change partners between Game 1 and Game 2.

  • Add the points each individual scored across both games.
  • Top 2 performers on a court stay/move up.
  • Bottom 2 stay/move down.
  • Incoming players are mixed with the holdovers; players who continuously remain at the same level do not immediately re-partner with each other.
  • The final top court produces one individual Shootout Champion.
Recommended: Shootout can run long. Rally scoring or games to 7 keep stages moving.
🎲

Random Mixer

Casual

Partners and opponents are shuffled with repeat avoidance. Great when variety matters more than seeding.

SCORING

Traditional vs rally scoring

Traditional / side-out

Only the serving team can score a point.

  • If the serving team wins the rally, they score 1 point and continue serving.
  • If the receiving team wins the rally, the score does not change. They only win the serve.
  • Normal doubles service order applies.
  • Traditional games are commonly played to 11 and usually require win by 2, unless your group chooses otherwise.

Example: Team A is serving at 4-2. Team B wins the rally. The score stays 4-2, and Team B gets the serve.

PicklePlay Rally Scoring (Fixed-Side)

Every rally awards a point, no matter who served.

  • The team that wins the rally scores 1 point.
  • The team that wins the rally also serves the next rally.
  • Players stay on their starting left/right side for the entire game. They do not switch sides during the game.
  • If a team's score is even, the right-side player serves.
  • If a team's score is odd, the left-side player serves.
  • Games end at the selected score (such as 7 or 11) and are win by 1.

Example: Team A leads 4-2. Team B wins the rally, so the score becomes 4-3. Team B now serves, and because 3 is odd, Team B's left-side player serves. Team B wins the next rally, so the score becomes 4-4. Team B serves again, and because 4 is even, Team B's right-side player serves. Team B's players never switch sides.

Why use it? Games usually finish faster and are easier to time, which works especially well for Shootout.

This is PicklePlay's simplified fixed-side rally-scoring format. Some clubs or organizations may use a different rally-scoring system. If your group wants a house variation, agree on it before starting and use it consistently for the whole session.
